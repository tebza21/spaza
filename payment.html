PAYMENT PROTECTION

Secure payment for customers and sellers

Spaza will use a regulated payment provider for the actual payment. Spaza will never ask this website to store your card or online-banking credentials. The marketplace records the provider's verified transaction reference and uses it to drive the order, commission, refund and seller-payout records.

🔐

Provider-hosted payment

Card and banking authentication happens with the selected payment provider, not inside Spaza's database.

🧾

Traceable transaction

Payment, order, invoice and provider references remain linked so every financial event can be audited.

🛡️

Verified webhooks

An order becomes paid only after the provider's server-side notification is validated. A browser redirect alone will never mark an order paid.

Important: live payment processing is not connected yet. This screen is the approved architecture for the next integration step; it does not collect or charge payment information.

How the payment will work

1 · Checkout

Customer confirms cart

Spaza calculates the order total, seller fulfilments and applicable delivery/tax amounts.

2 · Payment provider

Customer pays securely

Spaza creates one payment attempt with a unique idempotency key and sends the customer to the configured provider.

3 · Verification

Provider confirms payment

A server-side webhook is checked for authenticity, amount, currency, reference and duplicate delivery.

4 · Order

Order becomes paid

Only verified payment changes the payment/order state. The order and seller fulfilments receive their traceable references.

5 · Marketplace accounting

Commission is recorded

Spaza records the configured commission and the resulting seller payable using the double-entry accounting design.

6 · After-sales

Refunds and payouts stay linked

Refunds, disputes and seller payouts reference the original payment/order instead of deleting or rewriting financial history.
